## Question

What are the achievement categories, rules, and progression semantics? Decide:

- Categories: streaks, consistency/recovery, roadmap milestones (complete/abandon), material completion, assessment performance, mastery, breadth vs depth.
- One-time awards vs levelable achievements (bronze/silver/gold, tree stages, tiers).
- Earn-and-keep vs losable achievements; how the existing streak behaves.
- How the learner sees criteria and progress toward the next achievement.
- Determinism and explainability: every achievement derivable from events + projections, never a manual claim.

## Context

Streak data already exists in `packages/progress` (`streak.ts`); progress projections drive Home/Week/Roadmaps. Achievements must be derived projections over the same data, rendered in the Marginalia visual language, mobile-first. This ticket feeds both the mastery/evidence contract and the badge UX prototype.
