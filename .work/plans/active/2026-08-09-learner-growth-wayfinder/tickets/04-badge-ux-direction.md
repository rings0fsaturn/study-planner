## Question

How should achievement feedback look and behave? Prototype and compare at least:

- Badge collection (cabinet of earned awards).
- Progress/growth tree that grows with study and mastery.
- Skill-growth map (per-material or per-skill bars/rings).
- Milestone timeline.
- A hybrid (cabinet + skill growth).

Cover where it surfaces (Home, a dedicated cabinet/achievements surface, session end), locked vs unlocked states, progress toward the next achievement, mobile + desktop, reduced motion, accessibility, and understandable copy.

## Context

The current streak feedback is a small card on Home (`StreakCard.tsx`). The prototype decides the visual language before the achievement model and the share cards commit to it.
