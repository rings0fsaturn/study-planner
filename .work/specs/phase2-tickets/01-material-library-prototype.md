## What to build

Create a throwaway, clickable prototype for the Material Library and its contextual material-picker flows. Explore list-first browsing, material detail, source creation, ingestion progress, archive/restore, replace-keep-id, stale dependent content, delete-referenced-by-roadmap messaging, multi-material selection, and the "Practice this" entry point.

The prototype must cover mobile and desktop states and make the unresolved interaction choices visible for review. It must not implement production persistence or ingestion.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The prototype includes library, detail, create, progress, failed/retry, archive/restore, stale, and referenced-delete states.
- [ ] The prototype includes contextual multi-material pickers and the "Practice this" flow.
- [ ] The prototype is clickable at mobile and desktop widths and records the recommended choice for implementation.
- [ ] The prototype preserves the approved server-first material model and ready-only generation rule.
