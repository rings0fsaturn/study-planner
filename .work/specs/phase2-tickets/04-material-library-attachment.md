## What to build

Deliver the first production vertical slice of the Material Library. A learner can create, list, inspect, archive, restore, and attach contentless materials to an assessment or Practice entry through the approved server-owned model.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Material records are owner-scoped and support the approved source types and contentless planning state.
- [ ] Library, detail, archive/restore, and responsive picker flows work on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] Assessment and Practice entry points can attach one or more materials without duplicating material entities.
- [ ] Cross-user access and account switching are covered by tests.
