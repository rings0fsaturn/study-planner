## What to build

Create a throwaway, clickable prototype for how mastery and adaptive recommendations surface in Roadmap and Progress views. Explore advisory signals, uncertainty, one-band difficulty movement, model-version context, stale/rebuildable feedback, and the boundary between recommendations and pinned roadmap decisions.

The prototype must not invent a second mastery model or silently change roadmap bookings.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The prototype shows cold-start uncertainty, updated mastery, adaptive recommendation, stale projection, and rebuild states.
- [ ] The prototype clearly distinguishes advisory feedback from pinned roadmap decisions.
- [ ] The prototype is clickable at mobile and desktop widths and records the recommended choice for implementation.
- [ ] The prototype consumes the approved mastery and adaptive-recommendation vocabulary without redefining its algorithm.
