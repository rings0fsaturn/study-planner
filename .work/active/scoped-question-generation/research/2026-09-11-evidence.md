# Evidence — generation drift and page-outline feasibility (2026-09-11)

All numbers below were measured against the live dev stack on 2026-09-11 (dev Supabase project, local ROCm sidecar on :8200, material `ACCA APM Study Text`, 572 pages, 754 chunks). Recorded here so the design rests on measurements rather than impressions, and so the numbers survive even if the probe scripts are removed.

## 1. The defect — what the generator has been producing

Query: `content_chunks` → `questions` (service role), ordered by `created_at`.

- **25 questions, all from the one ready material. 25/25 ask about the examination or the document; 0 about the material's technical content.**
- 16 written / 9 objective; **22 distinct skill-tag sets** — tag input does not control the subject of the question.
- Representative prompts:
  - "In the ACCA APM exam, what is the total duration of the computer-based examination?"
  - "In the ACCA APM exam, where are the professional skills marks allocated?"
  - "Describe the structure of the ACCA APM examination, including the duration, total marks, pass mark, and the composition of Section A..."
  - "Explain how the APM syllabus is structured in terms of technical content and professional skills, and describe the format of Section A of the exam." (the user's report)

## 2. The mechanism — retrieval returns front matter

Reproduced with `scripts/diagnose_generation_context.py` (sidecar query embedding, `is_query=True`, then `match_content_chunks` with `top_k = 5`, exactly as `generation/context.py` does).

| steer | retrieved chunk ordinals |
|---|---|
| `ACCA APM Study Text, knowledge, comprehension` | **0** (cover), **753** (index), **67** (chapter-1 boundary), **24** ("The examination / Approach to examining the syllabus"), **30** ("Study skills and revision guidance") |
| `ACCA APM Study Text` | 0, 753, 67, 24, 49 |
| `ACCA APM Study Text, core` | 0, 753, 67, 24, 49 |
| `core` | 59, 344 ("necessary."), 532 ("369"), 6, 90 |
| `gap analysis` | 114, 115 (F0/F1/F2 planning gap), 96, 97, 731 |
| `decision-making techniques` | 131, 132 (maximax), 729, 730 |
| `performance measurement and control` | 52, 51, 47, 4, 13 |

- **5/5 retrieved chunks are front or back matter** for every steer that contains the title, while only **64 of 754 chunks (8.5%)** are exam-meta. A blind draw would place ~0.4 such chunks in a 5-chunk context.
- Removing the title is what changes the outcome: the topic alone retrieves the technical chapters.
- Code path: `generation/context.py:30` (`steer = title + skill tags`), reused as the prompt steer at `generation/prompts.py:145` and `:200` and as the BM25 `query_text` at `context.py:44`.
- Default tag is `["core"]` (`routers/assessments.py:29`), so an unscoped request still injects a meaningless term.

Conclusion: this is retrieval bias, not corpus shape, and not a model failure. The prompt asks for a question grounded strictly in the given chunks; the given chunks are the cover, the contents page and the study guidance.

## 4. Outline parser prototype

`scripts/pdf_outline.py` — deterministic contents-page scan, optional DeepSeek fallback via the service's own OpenRouter adapter, `--self-check` for the assertions.

| PDF | Pages | Contents page | Entries | Source | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| APM study text | 572 | pdf 6 | 16 | deterministic | 1.4 s |
| SICP | 883 | pdf 5 | 91 | deterministic | — |
| CSAPP (2016) | 1105 | pdf 3 | 128 | deterministic | — |
| DDIA | 613 | pdf 9 | 188 | deterministic | — |
| APM study text | 572 | pdf 6 | 16 (exact) | DeepSeek, forced | 16.2 s |

Four publishers, four TOC layouts, zero model calls on the deterministic path. Three layout rules were discovered by reading the real extracted text (all pinned by `--self-check`):

1. Dot leaders may be space-separated (`1.1 The Elements of Programming . . . . . . . . 6`) — a naive `\.{2,}` misses them.
2. Publishers wrap long titles, so the title, its tail and its page number can be three separate lines (`Chapter 6 Business structure and performance` / `management` / `181`).
3. Some layouts put a single space before the page number (`1.1 Information Is Bits + Context 39`); that rule is only enabled once the page says "Contents", or it over-matches body prose.

Model-vs-parser note: the LLM returns titles without the `Chapter N` prefix, so the two lists must be compared by page number, never by title substring.

Also worth knowing when tuning: `--front` window 40 is enough for all four PDFs, and the DeepSeek call's `contentsPageIndex` agreed with the deterministic scan on the APM corpus.
