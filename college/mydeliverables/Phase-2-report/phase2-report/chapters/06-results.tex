\chapter{Results and Discussion}
This chapter reports the offline comparison for each Phase-II loop stage and discusses what the numbers mean for the delivered system. All offline numbers run on the fixed artefacts described in Section~5.2, under the comparison design of Section~4.3. The metrics are those of Table~\ref{tab:phase2-metrics}; this chapter reports against them without redefining them. Section~6.1 reports generation quality with the retrieval, generation, and tracing tables. Section~6.2 reports live verification as a deployment check. Section~6.3 reports the closed-loop verdict. \section{Generation Quality}
The offline leg scores components against fixed golds behind the fail-closed gate of Figure~\ref{fig:comparison-pipeline}. Three tables carry the leg: retrieval with ranking, generation with its grading signals, and tracing with calibration. Reproducibility for both legs rests on Section~5.4. The harness stays inert unless activated, every call carries one request identifier, and both legs re-run from versioned files.

Table~\ref{tab:res-retrieval} reports recall and MRR on the coherent 754-chunk ACCA split, the live endpoint row, and the DDIA second corpus. The harness dense and hybrid arms reproduce the recorded in-process baselines exactly, which validates the rerank arm beside them. Reranking lifts hybrid recall at 1 by 7 points (0.60 to 0.67) and recall at 3 by 10 points (0.80 to 0.90). MRR rises by 0.077 (0.720 to 0.797). The live endpoint reproduces the plain RPC arm exactly (0.60/0.73/0.87/0.703) and its reranked row reads 0.70/0.90/0.93/0.813. It is to be noted that the endpoint reranked row sits above the harness arm because the RPC candidate set differs from the in-process copy. The DDIA corpus moves the same direction with larger magnitude. Reranking lifts recall at 1 by 16 points (0.47 to 0.63) and recall at 3 by 17 points (0.73 to 0.90). MRR rises by 0.139 (0.660 to 0.799). Cross-corpus absolutes are not comparable because the two gold sets were authored independently; only the arm deltas transfer. The retired 788-split headline (recall at 1 of 0.77, recall at 3 of 0.97, MRR of 0.858) is the frozen-split precedent only. Two rejected arms stay on record: title-prefix indexing shows no gain, and int8 quantisation collapses to 0.10 across recalls at 2.45 times speedup.

\section{Live Verification}
The live matrix confirms deployment and never promotes a candidate, per Section~4.3. Objective runs take 2 of 2, written runs grade 4 of 4 with real rubric grades, and coding runs grade 3 of 3. Guide runs pass 2 of 2, practice runs pass 2 of 2, and feedback copy passes 3 of 3. Mastery passes 2 of 2 and review passes 4 of 4. Ingestion passes 5 with 2 skipped. Every run reports zero console and page errors. AC1, AC3, and AC4 pass with AC2 passing with skips. Each call joins frontend to service logs on one request identifier per Section~5.4.

At scale, the full-book E2E runs through the sidecar. The 572-page fixture (about 23 MB) moves from upload to created at 13.5 s and extracting at 15 s. Embedding reads 48 s and ready reads 101 s. The start-to-ready wall is about 88 s at 10 s poll granularity. The same fixture yields the durable 754-chunk baseline with 0 NULL embeddings. The material was deleted after the run with the card-gone state verified, so the shared account sits as found.

\section{Synthetic Longitudinal Validation}
The longitudinal leg replays four synthetic archetypes with known posture: steady, crammer, struggling, and returning. Development histories stay separate from evaluation histories, so a conversion reflects behaviour instead of fitting. The full 24-row adherence emission and the per-user decidedAt values sit in Appendix~\ref{ch:appendix}; this section reports the headline with its caveat.

Next-week adherence measures 0.000 for all four in the post-replan extension week. Capture ran one day before the deadline. No sessions were logged after the replan, so the zero is a measured zero with that rebound-era caveat, not a learning result. The 12 mastery rows read after at about 0.1067 throughout, below the prior of 0.15 by construction. The seeded scripts answer about 90 percent wrong because they prioritise grading-path coverage over learner success. Those rows measure the scripts and are never cited as tracing validation; the tracing evidence lives in Table~\ref{tab:res-bkt}. Each of the 4 verdicts joins its \texttt{RoadmapReplanned} by the exact verdict pair with createdAt at or after decidedAt. A seed-shape finding is recorded with the evidence. The seed writes one timestamp with a zone offset while the booking reference uses milliseconds. The strict-equality join silently mistraces until emission canonicalises the instant. The product is untouched.

The dip-window replay pins the browser clock to the week-3 trough for every archetype. All four hold at confidence 0.28 to 0.35 with sufficiency 0.66 to 0.77 and convert nothing, because \texttt{Keep} writes nothing. A September-12 pin for the struggling archetype flips the choice to \texttt{suggest\_replan} at 0.61 to 0.69 but the gate holds it. The gradient reads as intended: trough holds on the merits, week 4 proposes below the gate, and the rebound eve converts at 1.0. The replay drove verdicts only and produced no new graded attempts, so the difficulty pool is unchanged. Cloud counts sit unchanged after all runs with no new writes and no synced markers. Four caveats travel with this leg. The clock pins to 2026-10-14, and capture covers the rebound era rather than the week-3 dip. The onboarding gate is local only, and cold restore duplicates locally while extracting from cloud only.

\section{Discussion}
Read together, the results separate into honest nulls, real wins, and validated refinements not yet shipped. Tracing is a null on ranking and a real improvement on calibration. AUC holds at parity while the calibration error nearly halves on live attempts. Coding on the golds is an honest terminal, not a failure. The arm refuses rather than invents, and the live pull passes its gate. Difficulty calibration and next-week adherence are unmeasured and measured-zero respectively, each with its named cause rather than a softened adjective. The clear wins are four. Retrieval ranking rises on both corpora in the same direction. Grounded generation reads 0.8667 with near-duplicates at 0.0044. The verdict converts 4 of 4 with its pre-fix contrast. The full-book path reaches ready in about 88 s.

Two refinements stay validated but unshipped, carried from Phase~I: the split-conformal interval correction and the single-arm CUSUM posture. Neither enters the delivered behaviour, and both return in Chapter~7 as numbered future work. Transfer holds in the sense the design needs. Dense and hybrid reproduce their baselines exactly on the rerun, which validates the rerank arm. The DDIA corpus repeats the ACCA direction at larger magnitude. The limits are stated plainly. The scope is a single learner with synthetic histories in place of a classroom study. One difficulty question is eligible, with the four replay caveats of Section~6.4. The loop that stayed dashed in Phase~I runs solid here. Figure~\ref{fig:architecture} shows where each stage from Table~\ref{tab:method-candidates} now runs.
