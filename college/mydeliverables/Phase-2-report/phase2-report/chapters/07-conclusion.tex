\chapter{Conclusion and Future Work}
This chapter closes the report. Section~7.1 concludes what the two phases delivered. Section~7.2 describes future work.

\section{Conclusion}
Phase~I built an open-loop adaptive planner for a single self-directed learner. Phase~I combined a Bayesian pace estimate and enriched shrinkage with CUSUM shift detection. It projected completion with a Gaussian process and a cold-start composite, then regenerated the plan with capacity bookings. Evaluation reported nulls on retrospective recovery error and change detection, and wins on prospective calibration and scheduling.

Phase~II closed the loop with assessment-based verification of graded evidence. The project's main contribution is the verified-learning closed loop, which extends the predict-then-optimize idea~\cite{islam2024} from predicted grades to graded evidence from the learner's own answers.

The closest prior is the Cognitive Tutor line~\cite{anderson1995,koedinger2007}. It combines step tracing, knowledge tracing, and mastery learning, reviewed at near-human tutoring effects~\cite{vanlehn2011}. This project narrows that architecture to one self-directed learner without a classroom. It uses generated grounded items instead of authored curricula, per-family server grades instead of step tracing, and a verdict-driven replan instead of a teacher. Logged time is self-reported, so it cannot evidence understanding. Graded responses come from a server-authoritative arm, with the guidance ladder gating the answer, though help-seeking can still be gamed~\cite{gaming2026}.

Table~\ref{tab:res-retrieval} shows retrieval gains on both corpora, with the dense and hybrid arms reproducing their recorded baselines. Reranking lifts hybrid recall at 1 from 0.60 to 0.67 on the 754-chunk ACCA split and from 0.47 to 0.63 on the DDIA corpus. MRR rises by 0.077 on ACCA and by 0.139 on DDIA, while the live endpoint is 0.813 reranked. On the retired 788 split, title-prefix indexing shows no gain, and int8 quantization collapses to 0.10. The retired 788-split headline stays as frozen-split precedent only.

Grounded generation measures 0.8667 on the judge against 0.7803 gold support, with schema validity at 0.9611 and citation validity at 0.9133. The two metrics measure different things. The coding arm refuses material it cannot ground, so derivability on the golds reads 0.0 on the pre-top-up run. A fresh live pull derives at 0.5349 over 43 judged attempts (0.371 over all 62 attempts). The 0.5 gate clears only on the judged pair.

Tracing holds at parity on ranking while calibration nearly halves on live attempts. bkt-v1 measures 0.7397 against 0.7368 for the running proportion over 282 observations, with ECE at 0.1401 against 0.2679. The shipped parameters hold mean AUC at 0.785 with ECE at 0.057, and the external anchor gives 0.602 to 0.611. Difficulty calibration stays unmeasured, with 23 observations on a single question in one band.

Live verification confirms deployment only, with objective runs at 2 of 2, written grades at 4 of 4, and coding grades at 3 of 3. The full-book path moves from created to ready in about 88~s (101~s from upload start) for the 572-page fixture.

The loop verdict converts 4 of 4 \texttt{suggest\_replan} verdicts into verdict-linked replans, as Table~\ref{tab:res-verdict} records. Confidence measures 1.0 with sufficiency from 0.66 to 0.77, and all four synthetic replays extended the deadline over a 32-day span. The pre-fix contrast is 4 of 4 hold at 0.18 to 0.34, and the gate moved from 0.9 to 0.7.

Synthetic longitudinal replay leaves next-week adherence unobserved, since capture ended one day before the deadline, and the 24-cell adherence emission sits in Appendix~\ref{ch:appendix}. The dip-window replay holds 4 of 4 at 0.28 to 0.35. The separate Oct-14 rebound-eve run converts at 1.0. The only validated refinement to a shipped component that stays unshipped is the split-conformal correction.

Together, the loop demonstrates graded evidence steering a plan on synthetic histories with no fixed-plan control. Retrieval grounds the questions, the rubric and sandbox arms grade them, and the verdict converts the grades into a replan. Learning gain stays undemonstrated: no run measured it, and it remains future work.

\section{Future Work}
Section~5.4 records the versioned artifacts that make both legs reproducible. The appendix carries the full adherence and verdict tables. Two near-term lines follow. The first is to ship the validated refinement: the split-conformal correction of the projection interval, which Section~6.5 leaves as the deferred calibration fix. The second is difficulty calibration with two or more bands, so the adaptive chip moves from advisory to measured. It needs more eligible questions than the single band available here, and a thicker met-class than the current two pairs.

Two longer-term lines close the report. Longitudinal validation on real data compares closed-loop planning against the fixed-plan baseline on adherence and on a pre-registered assessment of learning gain. A single-learner study over one term is the next step beyond synthetic histories, and it answers the rebound-era capture caveat of Section~6.4. The smallest usability gate before that study is an expert heuristic review or a 3-to-5 learner pilot. No usability evidence exists yet. Classroom scope extends the single-learner loop to groups without losing per-learner mastery.

\section{Limitations}
The limits of Section~6.6 stand as the closing word. The evidence is synthetic and single-learner. The groundedness judge grades its own generator over six questions. The coding gate clears only on the judged pair, difficulty calibration is unmeasured, and week 5 is unobserved.

No fixed-plan control ran, so no efficacy comparison exists. Learner material travels to a third party with no stated retention contract, the sandbox runs without a stated resource bound, and prompt injection is untested. The contribution is a working integration of graded evidence into planning, not measured learning gain.
