What went wrong and what we changed
Challenge 1
Quota burst stopped the first full book

27,000 tokens in 10 seconds hit the per-minute limit. Throttle, resume-on-retry, and stage telemetry fixed it.

Challenge 2
Search quality halved overnight

Query and passages used different embedding models. One shared local encoder fixed both sides. Restore tooling now rebuilds corpora safely.

Challenge 3
Provider JSON mode lied twice

Routed endpoints drifted on 4 of 383 outputs. Local validation became mandatory.

Review 1 promised a full practice loop in two weeks. We built and hardened the content half. The assessment half starts now.
